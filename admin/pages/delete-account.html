HARZO Account Deletion

If you would like to permanently delete your HARZO account and associated personal data, please contact us using the details below.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email from your registered email address or mention your registered mobile number.
  2. Use the subject: Delete My HARZO Account.
  3. We may verify your identity before processing the request.

Contact

Email: support@harzo.in

What data will be deleted?

  • Your HARZO account
  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Saved addresses
  • Profile information

What data may be retained?

Order history, invoices, and records that must be retained to comply with applicable laws may be kept for the legally required period.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are normally processed within 7 business days.
